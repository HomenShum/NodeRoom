# Official Score Boundary

Product-path ProofLoop receipts can prove that the app, agent loop, local graph, memory, reports, and provider setup behave correctly. They do not become official benchmark scores by wording.

Official-score claims require an imported upstream scorer, Harbor/Gandalf receipt, or benchmark-accepted judge receipt. Proxy model sweeps, local deterministic gates, LangSmith mirrors, and product UI runs remain product proof unless the upstream benchmark accepts that path.

Launch copy should use:

- Product proof: local/live ProofLoop receipts, chart packs, dashboard exports, model/cost receipts.
- Official proof: upstream scorer or judge receipt stored under `docs/eval/proofloop-official-scores/`.
- Blocked official proof: explicit `blocked_external` status with the missing scorer, credential, task bundle, or acceptance path named.
