# NodeRoom Privacy Policy Draft

This draft is not published legal advice and requires owner/legal review.

NodeRoom processes account identity, room content, uploaded files, source captures, agent requests, generated artifacts, traces, and operational metadata to provide collaborative workrooms. The published policy must identify service providers, retention periods, access controls, deletion procedures, international processing, contact details, and user rights applicable to the launch audience.

Private room content must not appear in public rooms, launch samples, screenshots, evaluation bundles, or public operations dashboards. Secrets, authentication credentials, signed URLs, and private source URLs are excluded from public receipts.

Initial pilot users must not upload confidential banking data, regulated personal data, secrets, or MNPI unless NodeRoom has explicitly approved the data class and controls.
