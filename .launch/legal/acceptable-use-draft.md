# NodeRoom Acceptable Use Draft

Users may not use NodeRoom to violate law, platform rules, intellectual-property rights, privacy, or security; distribute malware or spam; evade authentication or rate limits; expose credentials; or make unreviewed high-impact financial, lending, employment, housing, insurance, legal, or medical decisions.

The initial pilot excludes confidential banking data, secrets, regulated personal data, and MNPI unless NodeRoom explicitly approves the data class and controls.

NodeRoom may pause jobs, providers, workspaces, distribution, or production writes when abuse, spend, privacy, security, or reliability thresholds are breached.
