# Maker Comment Draft

I built NodeRoom because important company work rarely starts as a clean prompt. It starts as a conversation, a spreadsheet, a few links, and half-finished notes.

NodeRoom gives that work a shared place to become inspectable. A human and NodeAgent can work in the same room, create source-backed spreadsheet and notebook artifacts, see evidence and execution traces, review proposed changes, and export the result without silent overwrites.

For this release we are focusing on finance and VC research workflows rather than claiming a general AI operating system or complete Excel compatibility. The sample room is synthetic and instant. Live workflows are bounded and reviewable.

Please visit, try the workflow, and tell us specifically where the product is unclear, slow, or untrustworthy. Detailed comments are more useful to us than promotion.
