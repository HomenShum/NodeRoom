# Product Hunt FAQ Draft

## What is NodeRoom?

A shared workroom where humans and NodeAgent turn company research inputs into source-backed spreadsheet, notebook, graph, evidence, trace, and export artifacts.

## Is the sample real customer data?

No. The launch sample is synthetic, deterministic, clearly labeled, and does not consume provider credits.

## Does live mode call AI providers?

Yes. Live mode requires an account and must display the estimated cap, job status, and an honest partial or failure state. Provider and spend controls must pass before public launch.

## Is this a full Excel replacement?

No. Spreadsheet support is focused on the supported research and workpaper workflows. Full Excel formula, formatting, macro, and chart compatibility is not claimed.

## Are benchmark scores official?

Only results produced by an accepted official runner and scorer may be described as official. Product-path and benchmark-faithful browser proof are labeled separately.

## Can I upload confidential banking data or MNPI?

Not during the initial public-data pilot unless NodeRoom explicitly approves the data class and applicable controls. Users should not upload secrets, regulated data, confidential banking data, or MNPI to the pilot.

## Can I delete my data or report a problem?

The published launch must include visible support and deletion-request paths. Those paths remain a launch gate until tested end to end.
