# Product Hunt Launch-Day Plan

Status: draft; no submission or scheduling is approved.

## Before Scheduling

1. Product Hunt gate passes against the production URL.
2. Submission, media, links, privacy, terms, support, public repositories, and monitoring are complete.
3. `productHuntSubmissionApproved` and any required distribution flags are true in local approval state.
4. Support and production operators confirm availability.

## Planned Window

- Standard planned-launch target: 12:01 a.m. Pacific on the owner-approved date.
- This is Product Hunt's current rule of thumb, not a ranking guarantee; choose a time when product and support operators are staffed.
- Product Hunt currently allows scheduling within 30 days. Recheck official guidance immediately before scheduling.

## Launch

- Maker submits or schedules the product.
- Distribution asks people to visit, try, comment, and give feedback. Never ask directly for upvotes.
- Do not mass-message, incentivize, or coordinate voting.
- Monitor production, queue, provider health, spend, first-stream latency, completion, exports, incidents, and substantive comments.

## After Launch

- Answer substantive questions with supported facts.
- Record feedback in the NodeRoom launch room.
- Publish fixes, limitations, and incidents honestly.
- Report activated rooms, return users, successful exports, and cost without fabricating adoption or testimonials.
