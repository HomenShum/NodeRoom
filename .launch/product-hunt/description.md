# NodeRoom

NodeRoom turns rough company conversations, notes, and files into source-backed workpapers while humans and AI work together in one live room.

Start with a company-research task. NodeAgent gathers sources, structures companies, people, and events, and builds reviewable spreadsheet, notebook, graph, evidence, and trace artifacts. You can inspect what changed, accept or reject governed proposals, and export the result instead of trusting an invisible answer.

The launch experience includes an instant synthetic sample that uses no model credits. Live work requires an account and is bounded by the supported workflow and visible job controls.

NodeRoom is launching first for finance, VC, accounting, GTM, and company-research workflows. It is a beta workroom, not a claim of full Excel compatibility or an official benchmark score.
