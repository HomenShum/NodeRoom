# NodeRoom Pilot And Launch Support

Status: draft; owner and support channel require approval.

## Supported Pilot Work

- Source-backed company, person, and event diligence.
- Runway and milestone research.
- Bank reconciliation and trial-balance workflows using synthetic or explicitly approved non-confidential data.
- Governed spreadsheet, notebook, graph, evidence, trace, review, and export flows.

## User Requests

- Product issue: capture room URL only when the user approves, visible error, timestamp, job ID, and non-sensitive trace receipt.
- Deletion request: verify account identity, identify rooms/files covered, acknowledge the request, and record completion without retaining deleted content in support notes.
- Privacy/security report: escalate immediately and do not request secrets, passwords, OAuth codes, or raw private files.
- Billing/credits: provide reservation, settled amount, job cap, and receipt; never infer cost from UI text alone.

## Launch Coverage

- Name one primary and one backup support owner before private pilot approval.
- Review pilot traces daily and launch monitoring every 15 minutes during the public launch window.
- Keep an operator available for migration, rollback, provider kill switch, and Product Hunt responses.

## Exit Criteria

The support gate remains blocked until the owner, channel, response targets, deletion procedure, and escalation drill are recorded in a production receipt.
