## What the company does
Quillmark advances cash against approved invoices that wholesale distributors issue to grocery chains and restaurant groups.
Distributors typically wait 45 to 60 days to be paid; Quillmark funds up to 85% of invoice face value within one business day.
Repayment comes from the distributor's customer through a controlled collection account that Quillmark administers.
Revenue is a discount fee on each funded invoice plus a monthly platform fee for the receivables dashboard.
Advances are funded through a senior warehouse facility; Quillmark retains a first-loss equity tranche.

## Products
- Quillmark Advance: single-invoice financing with same-day underwriting
- Quillmark Line: revolving receivables facility for distributors above $5M in annual revenue
- Collections Desk: outsourced collections and cash application for enrolled distributors
