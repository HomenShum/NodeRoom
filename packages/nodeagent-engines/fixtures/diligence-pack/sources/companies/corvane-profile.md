## What the company does
Corvane provides merchants with a lump-sum advance repaid as a fixed share of daily online sales.
Underwriting reads storefront sales, refunds and ad-spend data through read-only platform connections.
Repayment is swept from merchant payouts, so collections speed rises and falls with merchant sales.
Revenue is a fixed financing fee per advance, recognised over the expected repayment period.
Most merchants take a second advance within nine months of the first.

## Products
- Corvane Flex: revenue-based advances from $10K to $500K
- Corvane Inventory: purchase-order financing paid directly to suppliers
- Corvane Insights: free sales-forecasting dashboard used as an acquisition channel
