## What the company does
Pellucid provides an API that lets vertical software platforms offer working-capital loans to their merchants.
Partner platforms serve salons, veterinary clinics and fitness studios.
Pellucid underwrites, funds and services the loans; partners receive a revenue share.
Revenue is loan interest and fees net of partner revenue share.
The company is the fastest growing in the cohort and also the heaviest spender.

## Products
- Pellucid Capital API: embedded loan offers, underwriting and servicing
- Pellucid Pay Later: deferred-payment terms for merchant software subscriptions
- Partner Console: reporting and offer-targeting tools for software partners
