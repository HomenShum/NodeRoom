## What the company does
Halvorine lends to staffing agencies to cover weekly payroll while client invoices are outstanding.
Loans are short duration, typically 30 to 45 days, and repaid from client payments.
Underwriting uses timesheet and invoice data pulled from payroll-software integrations.
Revenue is a fee per payroll cycle funded plus interest on balances carried past 45 days.
The company is the oldest and largest by ARR in this cohort.

## Products
- Halvorine Payroll Bridge: weekly payroll funding up to 90% of approved invoices
- Halvorine Back Office: invoicing and collections service for staffing agencies
- Rate Desk: bill-rate benchmarking tool for agency sales teams
