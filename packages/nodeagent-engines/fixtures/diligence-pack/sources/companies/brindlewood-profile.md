## What the company does
Brindlewood funds subcontractors against progress billings and retainage owed by general contractors.
Subcontractors often wait 60 to 90 days for progress payments and longer for retainage release.
Lines are secured by assigned receivables and, where available, payment-bond rights.
Revenue is interest on drawn balances plus an annual line fee.
Underwriting scores both the subcontractor and the paying general contractor.

## Products
- Brindlewood Progress: revolving line against approved progress billings
- Brindlewood Retainage: advances against retainage held on completed work
- Lien Desk: lien-notice tracking for enrolled subcontractors
