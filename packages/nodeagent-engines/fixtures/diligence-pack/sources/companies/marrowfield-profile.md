## What the company does
Marrowfield finances seed, fertilizer and crop-protection purchases for growers through agricultural retailers.
Credit is drawn in spring and repaid after harvest, so balances and cash needs are seasonal.
Retail partners originate the loans at the point of sale and share in the economics.
Revenue is interest and an origination fee, recognised over the season.
Crop insurance assignments and grain-elevator payment directions support repayment.

## Products
- Marrowfield Season: input credit lines repaid after harvest
- Marrowfield Retail: retailer-branded financing program
- Harvest Sweep: direct payment from grain elevators against outstanding balances
