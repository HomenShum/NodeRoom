## What the company does
Tessaly finances kitchen equipment such as ovens, refrigeration and point-of-sale hardware for independent restaurants.
Loans run 24 to 48 months and are secured by the financed equipment.
Most loans originate at the point of sale through partner equipment dealers.
Revenue is interest income plus dealer referral fees; in 2026 the company also ran a paid pilot with a restaurant group.
Repossessed equipment is resold through a dealer network; recovery rates are tracked by equipment type.

## Products
- Tessaly Equip: 24 to 48 month equipment term loans from $10K to $150K
- Tessaly Refresh: refinancing of existing equipment leases
- Dealer Portal: point-of-sale application and instant decision tool for equipment dealers
