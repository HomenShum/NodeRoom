## What the company does
Ynderra provides fleets with credit lines for fuel, tires and maintenance, accessed through a fleet card.
Balances are billed weekly and repaid by direct debit.
Underwriting uses telematics and fuel-network transaction data.
Revenue is interchange-like transaction fees plus interest on balances carried past the billing cycle.
Fleet customers are larger than typical small-business borrowers, so each relationship carries more revenue.

## Products
- Ynderra Fleet Card: fuel and maintenance credit line with weekly billing
- Ynderra Repair Advance: term financing for major engine and transmission repairs
- Route Insights: fuel-spend analytics for fleet managers
