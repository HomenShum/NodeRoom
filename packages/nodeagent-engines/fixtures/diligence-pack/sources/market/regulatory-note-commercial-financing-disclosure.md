# Regulatory Note — Commercial Financing Disclosure (Illustrative)

Prepared for: Startup Banking diligence team, Q3 2026 credit committee
Document type: regulatory note (illustrative scenario)
As of: July 31, 2026
Status: current

## Important
- This note describes an illustrative, fictional regulatory scenario created for this exercise.
- It does not summarise any actual statute, regulation or guidance and must not be relied on as legal advice.

## Scenario
- A fictional multi-state model rule requires cost-of-credit disclosure for commercial financings below $500K.
- Required disclosures in the scenario: total cost of financing, estimated annualized cost, payment schedule and prepayment terms.
- The scenario rule takes effect for financings originated after January 1, 2027.

## Product impact (scenario)
- Revenue-based financing must present an estimated annualized cost using historical sales; affects Corvane Capital.
- Payroll bridge loans with per-cycle fees must disclose fee totals per cycle; affects Halvorine Funding.
- Embedded loans offered through partners require disclosure in the partner interface; affects Pellucid Ledger.
- Checkout trade credit with late fees must disclose maximum late-fee cost; affects Solvane Trade Credit.
- Equipment, invoice, fleet, agricultural and franchise term loans already disclose most required terms.

## Compliance cost (scenario estimates)
- One-time compliance build for affected lenders: $0.2M to $0.6M
- Ongoing compliance cost: under 1% of revenue for most affected lenders

## Timeline (scenario)
- Model rule published for comment: March 2026.
- Comment period closed: May 2026.
- Final scenario rule expected: October 2026.
- Effective date for new originations: January 1, 2027.

## Diligence questions
- Has the company mapped its products to the scenario's disclosure requirements?
- Who owns compliance and is there outside counsel review?
- Are partner platforms contractually responsible for displaying disclosures?
- Has the company budgeted the one-time compliance build in its 2026 operating plan?

## Committee implications
- Treat the scenario as a modest cost item, not a thesis-breaking risk, for all companies in the pack.
- Pending litigation or enforcement matters are a separate and more serious category; see company call notes.
