Prepared for: Startup Banking diligence team, Q3 2026 credit committee
Document type: credit conditions note (synthetic)
As of: July 31, 2026
Status: current

## Summary
- Small-business credit conditions softened modestly in the first half of 2026.
- Delinquencies rose in restaurants, construction and small trucking fleets.
- Staffing, agriculture and franchise borrowers were broadly stable.
- All figures in this note are synthetic sector indicators prepared for this exercise.

## Restaurants
- Synthetic sector delinquency index, June 2026: 118 (June 2025 = 100)
- Food-cost and wage inflation continue to compress margins for independent restaurants.
- Restaurants open less than two years show the steepest deterioration.

## Construction subcontractors
- Synthetic sector delinquency index, June 2026: 124 (June 2025 = 100)
- General contractors are stretching payables, pushing delays down to subcontractors.
- Retainage release times lengthened by roughly 20 days year over year.

## E-commerce merchants
- Synthetic sector delinquency index, June 2026: 112 (June 2025 = 100)
- Rising advertising costs reduced merchant contribution margins, especially in apparel.

## Trucking fleets
- Synthetic sector delinquency index, June 2026: 109 (June 2025 = 100)
- Freight rates remained soft; owner-operators are most exposed.

## Staffing agencies
- Synthetic sector delinquency index, June 2026: 101 (June 2025 = 100)
- Temporary-staffing volumes fell but agencies with diversified clients remain current.

## Agriculture and franchises
- Agriculture synthetic delinquency index, June 2026: 99 (June 2025 = 100)
- Franchise synthetic delinquency index, June 2026: 103 (June 2025 = 100)
- Drought pockets and commodity prices are the main agricultural watch items.

## Committee implications
- Apply heavier scrutiny to restaurant, construction and small-fleet exposures.
- Ask each company for vintage curves rather than point-in-time delinquency.
