## Purpose
- Sets the criteria the Q3 2026 credit committee uses to compare venture-debt candidates.
- Committee meeting date: September 17, 2026.

## Metrics required in the comparison table
- ARR (restated where diligence found non-recurring revenue).
- Year-over-year ARR growth (restated where applicable).
- Gross margin after funding cost and credit losses.
- Average monthly net burn.
- Cash and equivalents (unrestricted).
- Runway in months, computed as cash divided by average monthly net burn.
- Top risk, taken from the diligence call findings.

## Screening thresholds
- Minimum runway for an unconditional proceed: 12 months
- Runway between 9 and 12 months: proceed only with an equity commitment or liquidity covenant
- Runway below 9 months: decline unless new equity is committed
- Single-customer concentration guideline: 25% of ARR
- Preferred minimum gross margin: 45%

## Evidence rules
- Every figure in the committee table must cite the source document and line.
- Figures from a document marked SUPERSEDED are not admissible; use the superseding version.
- Exceptions found in diligence calls (for example a restatement) override company-reported figures.
- Documents in the restricted data room are not available to the diligence team; list them as unavailable rather than estimating their contents.
- Benchmarks in market notes are for context only and are never company metrics.

## Ranking
- Rank candidates by runway, longest first, then apply the screening thresholds.
- Flag any company with a concentration or restatement exception regardless of rank.
