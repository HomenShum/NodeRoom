## Why this note exists
- Two companies in the pack lend into sectors with distinctive seasonality and concentration patterns.
- Ynderra Lending serves regional trucking fleets; Marrowfield Credit serves growers and agricultural retailers.
- All figures below are synthetic sector indicators.

## Trucking fleets
- Regional fleets buy fuel weekly, so fuel-credit balances turn over quickly.
- Large less-than-truckload carriers can represent outsized revenue for a fleet lender.
- Synthetic fleet-count index, mid-2026 versus mid-2025: 96
- Owner-operators exit fastest when freight rates are soft.
- Telematics data allows near real-time monitoring of fleet activity.

## Concentration in fleet lending
- A single large carrier can exceed typical bank concentration guidelines on its own.
- Warehouse lenders commonly cap any one obligor at 10% to 20% of the eligible pool.
- Diligence should model the loss of the largest customer.

## Agricultural inputs
- Input credit is drawn in spring and repaid after harvest in the fall.
- Cash needs peak in the third quarter before harvest repayments arrive.
- Crop insurance and grain-elevator payment directions are standard credit supports.
- Synthetic regional drought-exposure index, July 2026: 1.3 (normal = 1.0)

## Seasonality and reporting
- Quarter-end cash for agricultural lenders can overstate liquidity going into Q3.
- Diligence should ask for weekly cash forecasts through harvest.
- Restricted reserves required by warehouse lenders should be excluded from cash.

## Committee implications
- Apply concentration covenants for fleet lenders with a dominant customer.
- Apply seasonal liquidity covenants for agricultural lenders with short runway.
