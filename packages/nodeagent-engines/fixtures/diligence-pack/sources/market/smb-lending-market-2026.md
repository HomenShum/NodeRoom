# Small-Business Lending Market Overview

Prepared for: Startup Banking diligence team, Q3 2026 credit committee
Document type: market note (synthetic research summary)
As of: July 31, 2026
Status: current

## Scope
- This note sizes a synthetic market for non-bank credit to US small businesses with revenue between $0.5M and $50M.
- All figures are illustrative estimates prepared for this exercise, not published statistics.
- Segments covered: receivables finance, revenue-based finance, equipment finance, working-capital lines and embedded lending.

## Market size (illustrative)
- Estimated non-bank small-business credit outstanding: $310B
- Estimated annual originations: $185B
- Fintech lenders' estimated share of originations: 14%
- Estimated growth in fintech originations, 2025 to 2026: 19%

## Segment notes
- Receivables finance: mature segment; fintechs win on speed and integration with accounting systems.
- Revenue-based finance: fast growth since 2021, but loss rates rose in 2025 vintages as ad costs climbed.
- Equipment finance: competitive with captive manufacturer programs; collateral supports recoveries.
- Working-capital lines for contractors: underserved; payment delays are the core risk.
- Embedded lending: highest growth; economics depend on software-partner revenue share.
- Fleet and fuel credit: dominated by fuel-card incumbents; telematics underwriting is the main wedge.
- Agricultural input credit: seasonal; competes with manufacturer finance and farm-credit lenders.
- Franchise lending: long tenors; correlated with brand health.

## Demand drivers
- Bank small-business lending tightened in 2025 and remained selective in 2026.
- Small businesses increasingly expect financing inside the software they already use.
- Payment-term stretching by large customers raises working-capital needs for suppliers.

## Supply drivers
- Warehouse lenders remained open to fintech originators with clean loss data.
- Equity investors favour lenders with a clear path to break-even over growth at any cost.
- Credit-fund buyers of whole loans returned in the first half of 2026.

## Implications for the committee
- Liquidity runway matters more than growth when equity markets are selective.
- Revenue quality (recurring versus one-time) should be tested for every company.
- Concentration risk is common in fleet, embedded and marketplace models.
