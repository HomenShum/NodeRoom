## Fleet, agriculture and franchise
- Fuel-card incumbents (fictional category): broad acceptance networks, lower credit limits.
- Farm-credit lenders (fictional category): low cost of funds, conservative underwriting.
- Brightline Franchise Lending (fictional): government-guaranteed loan specialist.

## Competitive takeaways
- Speed and data integrations are the main fintech advantages across segments.
- Incumbents usually win on cost of funds.
- Sustainable fintech lenders pair data advantages with diversified, renewable funding.
- Watch for platforms that can switch lending partners, which turns distribution into a dependency.
