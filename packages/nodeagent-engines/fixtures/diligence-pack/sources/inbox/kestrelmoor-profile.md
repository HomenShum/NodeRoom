## What the company does
Kestrelmoor lends to franchisees to fund build-out and opening costs for new units.
Loans run five to seven years and are secured by the franchisee's business assets.
Franchisors refer franchisees through preferred-lender programs.
Revenue is interest income plus origination fees amortized over the loan life.
Brands span quick-service restaurants, fitness studios and home services.

## Products
- Kestrelmoor Build: new-unit build-out term loans
- Kestrelmoor Remodel: remodel loans required by franchise agreements
- Unit Economics Lab: pro-forma tools shared with franchisors
