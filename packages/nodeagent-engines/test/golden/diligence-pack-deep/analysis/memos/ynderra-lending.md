# Ynderra Lending — credit memo, Q3 2026 credit committee

## Overview

- Ynderra Lending was founded in 2021 [src:sources/companies/ynderra-profile.md#L10] and is headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee [src:sources/companies/ynderra-profile.md#L11].
- Segment: fuel and maintenance credit lines for regional trucking fleets [src:sources/companies/ynderra-profile.md#L14].
- Employees: 79 [src:sources/companies/ynderra-profile.md#L12].
- Diligence-call recommendation: proceed with conditions [src:sources/diligence-calls/ynderra-call-notes.md#L60].

## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Customer concentration: the largest customer, a regional less-than-truckload carrier, accounts for 46% of ARR, far above the committee's 25% guideline, with a 90-day termination clause. [src:sources/diligence-calls/ynderra-call-notes.md#L49]
- Secondary risk: Freight-rate softness pressuring smaller fleets. [src:sources/diligence-calls/ynderra-call-notes.md#L50]
- Mitigant: The largest customer has a perfect payment record. [src:sources/diligence-calls/ynderra-call-notes.md#L51]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Equipment, invoice, fleet, agricultural and franchise term loans already disclose most required terms. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L22]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.

## Exceptions

- Customer concentration: the largest customer is 46% of ARR [src:sources/diligence-calls/ynderra-call-notes.md#L25], against the committee guideline of 25% of ARR [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L25]; the top three customers are 63% of ARR [src:sources/diligence-calls/ynderra-call-notes.md#L26].
- The largest customer's contract renews in March 2027 with a 90-day termination clause [src:sources/diligence-calls/ynderra-call-notes.md#L28].
- Committee condition proposed on the call: a concentration covenant and a plan to bring the largest customer below 30% of ARR [src:sources/diligence-calls/ynderra-call-notes.md#L61].
