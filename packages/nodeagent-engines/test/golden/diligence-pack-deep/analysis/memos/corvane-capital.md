## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Credit losses: the 2025 vintage is running 6.1% annualized net charge-offs versus a 4.5% plan, and repayment speed depends on merchant sales. [src:sources/diligence-calls/corvane-call-notes.md#L41]
- Secondary risk: Monthly burn is the second highest in the cohort and acquisition costs are rising. [src:sources/diligence-calls/corvane-call-notes.md#L42]
- Mitigant: Approval cut-offs were tightened in May 2026. [src:sources/diligence-calls/corvane-call-notes.md#L43]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Revenue-based financing must present an estimated annualized cost using historical sales; affects Corvane Capital. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L18]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.

## Exceptions

- No exception was recorded on the diligence call. ARR method confirmed: ARR is Q2 net revenue multiplied by four; financing fees are recognised over expected repayment, not at funding. [src:sources/diligence-calls/corvane-call-notes.md#L18]
