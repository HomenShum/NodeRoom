## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Unseasoned credit: only 14 months of vintage history, so the 2.6% annualized net charge-off rate has not been tested through a downturn. [src:sources/inbox/solvane-call-notes.md#L41]
- Secondary risk: Reliance on two marketplace partners for most volume. [src:sources/inbox/solvane-call-notes.md#L42]
- Mitigant: Short payment terms limit exposure duration. [src:sources/inbox/solvane-call-notes.md#L43]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Checkout trade credit with late fees must disclose maximum late-fee cost; affects Solvane Trade Credit. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L21]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.
