## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Warehouse refinancing: the $175.0M facility matures in March 2027 and the renewal term sheet is priced 75 bps wider, compressing gross margin. [src:sources/diligence-calls/quillmark-call-notes.md#L41]
- Secondary risk: Geographic concentration in nine Midwest states. [src:sources/diligence-calls/quillmark-call-notes.md#L42]
- Mitigant: Longest cash runway in the cohort at current burn. [src:sources/diligence-calls/quillmark-call-notes.md#L43]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Equipment, invoice, fleet, agricultural and franchise term loans already disclose most required terms. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L22]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.

## Exceptions

- No exception was recorded on the diligence call. ARR method confirmed: The CFO confirmed ARR is Q2 net revenue multiplied by four; no one-time items were included in Q2. [src:sources/diligence-calls/quillmark-call-notes.md#L18]
