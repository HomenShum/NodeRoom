# Tessaly Credit — credit memo, Q3 2026 credit committee

## Overview

- Tessaly Credit was founded in 2022 [src:sources/companies/tessaly-profile.md#L10] and is headquartered in Denver, Colorado [src:sources/companies/tessaly-profile.md#L11].
- Segment: equipment loans for independent restaurants [src:sources/companies/tessaly-profile.md#L14].
- Employees: 58 [src:sources/companies/tessaly-profile.md#L12].
- Diligence-call recommendation: decline pending new equity [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L65].

## Financials

As of June 30, 2026, in USD millions.

- ARR (restated on the diligence call, see Exceptions): $11.6M [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L29]
- Year-over-year ARR growth (restated): 40% [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L31]
- Gross margin, Q2 2026: 47% [src:sources/financials/tessaly-financials.md#L25]
- Average monthly net burn, Q2 2026: $1.6M [src:sources/financials/tessaly-financials.md#L37]
- Cash and equivalents, June 30, 2026: $12.8M [src:sources/financials/tessaly-financials.md#L38]
- Runway: 8.0 months, derived as cash / monthly burn from the two lines above.
- Burn stress test (analysis/sensitivity.json): 6.7 months if burn rises 20%, 10.0 months if burn falls 20%.
- Loans outstanding, June 30, 2026: $84.0M [src:sources/financials/tessaly-financials.md#L43]
- Loans 30+ days past due: 3.9% [src:sources/financials/tessaly-financials.md#L47]
- Annualized net charge-off rate, Q2 2026: 3.4% [src:sources/financials/tessaly-financials.md#L48]

## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Revenue quality and liquidity: reported ARR of $14.2M included $2.6M of annualized one-time pilot revenue (restated ARR $11.6M), and runway is the shortest in the cohort. [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L54]
- Secondary risk: Rising delinquency among restaurants open less than two years. [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L55]
- Mitigant: Equipment collateral with tracked recovery rates. [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L56]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Equipment, invoice, fleet, agricultural and franchise term loans already disclose most required terms. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L22]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.

## Exceptions

- ARR restated downward on the diligence call of July 21, 2026 [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L5].
- Reported ARR of $14.2M [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L26] included fees from a paid pilot: the pilot was a one-time implementation charge that ended in June 2026 and will not renew [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L24].
- One-time pilot revenue included in reported ARR (annualized): $2.6M [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L28].
- Restated ARR excluding one-time pilot revenue: $11.6M [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L29]; restated growth: 40% [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L31] (reported: 71% [src:sources/financials/tessaly-financials.md#L13]).
- This memo and the committee table use the restated figures [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L33].
- Board minutes discussing the pilot are unavailable to the diligence team [src:sources/diligence-calls/tessaly-call-notes.md#L34]: sources/restricted/tessaly-board-minutes-2026-06.md.
