## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Brand correlation: 31% of outstanding loans are to franchisees of two quick-service brands, so a downturn at either brand would hit roughly a third of the book. [src:sources/inbox/kestrelmoor-call-notes.md#L41]
- Secondary risk: Slower growth as franchise openings paused. [src:sources/inbox/kestrelmoor-call-notes.md#L42]
- Mitigant: Long operating history and low delinquency. [src:sources/inbox/kestrelmoor-call-notes.md#L43]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Equipment, invoice, fleet, agricultural and franchise term loans already disclose most required terms. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L22]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.

## Exceptions

- No exception was recorded on the diligence call. ARR method confirmed: ARR is Q2 net revenue multiplied by four; there were no one-time items. [src:sources/inbox/kestrelmoor-call-notes.md#L18]
