## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Construction payment delays: loans 30+ days past due rose to 4.4% from 2.9% a year earlier as general contractors stretched payables. [src:sources/diligence-calls/brindlewood-call-notes.md#L41]
- Secondary risk: Small collections team relative to rising delinquencies. [src:sources/diligence-calls/brindlewood-call-notes.md#L42]
- Mitigant: Payment-bond rights recover most delayed balances. [src:sources/diligence-calls/brindlewood-call-notes.md#L43]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Treat the scenario as a modest cost item, not a thesis-breaking risk, for all companies in the pack. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L41]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.
