## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Liquidity heading into the seasonal peak: after the v2 correction removed a $3.4M restricted reserve from cash and added omitted seasonal costs, runway is under nine months before the Q3 drawdown. [src:sources/diligence-calls/marrowfield-call-notes.md#L41]
- Secondary risk: Weather and commodity-price exposure concentrated in seven states. [src:sources/diligence-calls/marrowfield-call-notes.md#L42]
- Mitigant: Crop-insurance assignments on most balances. [src:sources/diligence-calls/marrowfield-call-notes.md#L43]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Equipment, invoice, fleet, agricultural and franchise term loans already disclose most required terms. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L22]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.
