## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Burn intensity and partner dependence: average monthly burn of $2.0M is the highest in the cohort and the top three software partners contribute 51% of originations. [src:sources/diligence-calls/pellucid-call-notes.md#L41]
- Secondary risk: Short loss history for recently launched partners. [src:sources/diligence-calls/pellucid-call-notes.md#L42]
- Mitigant: Largest cash balance in the cohort. [src:sources/diligence-calls/pellucid-call-notes.md#L43]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Embedded loans offered through partners require disclosure in the partner interface; affects Pellucid Ledger. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L20]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.
