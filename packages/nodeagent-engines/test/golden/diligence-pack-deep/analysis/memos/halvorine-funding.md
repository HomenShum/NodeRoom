## Risks

- Top risk from the diligence call: Undisclosed legal exposure and slowing growth: a pending legal matter could not be assessed because counsel's memo is restricted, and ARR growth slowed to 29% year over year. [src:sources/diligence-calls/halvorine-call-notes.md#L41]
- Secondary risk: Dependence on temporary-staffing volumes, which softened in 2026. [src:sources/diligence-calls/halvorine-call-notes.md#L42]
- Mitigant: Lowest delinquency in the cohort. [src:sources/diligence-calls/halvorine-call-notes.md#L43]
- Regulatory (illustrative scenario): Payroll bridge loans with per-cycle fees must disclose fee totals per cycle; affects Halvorine Funding. [src:sources/market/regulatory-note-commercial-financing-disclosure.md#L19]
- Committee screen: runway of at least 12 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L22] is needed for an unconditional proceed; below 9 months [src:sources/market/credit-committee-criteria-q3-2026.md#L24] is a decline unless new equity is committed.
