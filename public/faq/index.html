What is NodeRoom?

NodeRoom is a collaborative AI room where humans and NodeAgents work on shared artifacts with visible context and trace receipts.

Is NodeRoom a search-ranking tool?

No. Search performance should be measured through Search Console. NodeRoom SEO QA should not automate mass searches, fake clicks, or SERP scraping.

Are private rooms indexed?

Public marketing pages are intended to be crawlable. Room, demo, create, and app-scoped URLs are guarded with noindex behavior where appropriate.

What makes NodeRoom different from a chat assistant?

The room keeps artifacts, files, chat, agent actions, and trace evidence together so reviewers can inspect the result.
