What NodeRoom means

NodeRoom is a web app for collaborative AI work. It is not Node-RED, a blockchain node host, a music studio, or a generic room named after nodes.

What the app does

Teams use NodeRoom to keep chat, source-backed notes, spreadsheet changes, files, and agent receipts in one shared workspace.
