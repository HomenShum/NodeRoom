Comparison

NodeRoom is a live AI room around shared work, not a document with an assistant.

Notion AI is strong for document and workspace generation. NodeRoom emphasizes simultaneous human-agent collaboration, room codes, spreadsheets, trace receipts, and source-backed updates.
